About Me

I believe the best way to understand what I can do is to look at what I've built.

For the past year and a half, I've worked at the intersection of data, forecasting, and business decisions — not just running numbers, but sitting with clients and leadership teams, understanding the problem behind them, and turning data into something people can act on.

I've built forecasting systems that help predict demand more accurately and save real working hours every week. I've built dashboards that aren't meant to sit on a screen — they're used by planners, leaders, and clients to make decisions. That work earned me the Pinnacle Award, but the recognition matters less to me than the impact behind it.

Beyond my day job, I build because I'm curious. I've turned millions of search records into market insights, built analytical dashboards, explored customer and business behavior, and worked on projects simply because I wanted to understand how something works. Some started with a business problem. Others started with a question I couldn't stop thinking about.

I also write and build in public. I share what I learn, what I discover, and sometimes what I get wrong — because I'd rather let my work speak for me than fill a resume with words.

I'm Neel Chaniyara.
I don't collect skills to make a longer list. I learn, I build, I solve — and I put the work out there.

Resource Planning Analyst

TELUS Digital

My role sits where data meets operations. I work on workforce planning and forecasting — figuring out how much demand is coming, how much capacity is available, and how many people are actually needed to deliver the right customer experience without over- or under-staffing.

I work closely with Operations Managers, Business Partners, WFM leadership, and clients to turn operational data into staffing decisions. That means working with volume, AHT, shrinkage, occupancy, capacity, and staffing requirements across daily, weekly, monthly, and annual planning horizons.
